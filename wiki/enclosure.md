# Enclosure & Mechanical

## Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Model | Hammond 1593L |
| Dimensions | 100 x 60 x 25 mm |
| Material | Extruded aluminum, anodized |
| IP Rating | IP54 (splash resistant with gaskets) |
| Weight | ~85g (empty), ~130g (assembled) |
| Price | ~$4.00 |

## Required Cutouts

- **SMA Connector**: 6mm hole on short side
- **USB-C (Charging)**: 8x3mm slot on short side
- **OLED Window**: 30x12mm rectangle on front face
- **Switch Holes (x4)**: 6mm holes for tactile buttons

## Assembly Tips

- Mount PCB on standoffs or double-sided tape (3M VHB recommended)
- Use rubber grommets on switch holes for weather resistance
- Apply clear silicone sealant around SMA and USB cutouts
- Thermal pad between ESP32 and aluminum case for heat dissipation
- 18650 cell secured with foam padding to prevent vibration damage
