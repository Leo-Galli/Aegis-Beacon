# Emergency Cheat Sheet

In a crisis you do not read articles; you read one card. This page is that card: the emergency sequence in the fewest words that still work.

## The sequence

1. **STOP.** Assess. Is this an emergency?
2. **Beacon to EMERGENCY mode** (see [Mode Emergency](mode-emergency)).
3. **Confirm payload**: identity and coordinates (see [Config Payload Format](config-payload-format)).
4. **Report** if any channel works: MAYDAY, who, where, what (see [Mayday vs Pan Pan](mayday-vs-panpan)).
5. **Shelter, warmth, first aid** (see [Hypothermia Recognition](hypothermia-recognition)).
6. **Stay put**: a moving subject is unfindable (see [Incident Command](incident-command)).

## The signals

| Signal | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| SOS (Morse, repeating) | Distress, transmitted by the beacon |
| Six whistle blasts | Distress, for nearby searchers |
| Three whistle blasts | Reply: "we hear you" |
| Mirror flashes | Daytime long-range (see [Mirror Signaling](mirror-signaling)) |

## The numbers

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| The beacon's channel | Written on the case lid |
| Your callsign | In the payload |
| Your contacts | On the emergency card (see [Emergency Kit](emergency-kit)) |

## The one rule

The beacon transmits on schedule without you. Everything you do afterwards (report, shelter, signal) adds to that baseline; nothing replaces it.
