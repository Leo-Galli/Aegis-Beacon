# Cleaning and Inspection

The post-trip inspection is where the next trip's reliability is decided. This page is the routine: clean what needs cleaning, inspect what fails, replace what is worn.

## The cleaning routine

| Item | Clean with | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Case exterior | Damp cloth | Remove grit before it abrades |
| Antenna threads | Dry brush, dielectric grease renewal | Corrosion starts in the threads |
| Audio jack | Compressed air | Dust and grit |
| Buttons | Dry brush around the edges | Debris causes sticking (see [Button Troubleshooting](button-troubleshooting)) |
| Electronics | Dry, soft brush only | Never wet-clean the board unless drying fully (see [Water Damage Recovery](water-damage-recovery)) |

## The inspection list

| Sign | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Cracks in the case or seals | Replace: water will find them (see [Waterproofing](waterproofing-and-enclosure-sealing)) |
| Corrosion on any connector | Clean or replace now |
| Antenna kinks or cracks | Replace: broken conductor (see [Antenna Troubleshooting](antenna-troubleshooting)) |
| Loose screws | Retighten; check the thread is not stripped |
| Swollen battery | Remove and recycle immediately (see [Battery Troubleshooting](battery-troubleshooting)) |

## The after-rain special

After any wet trip: open the case, check for droplets, dry everything, and renew the grease on the seals and antenna threads. Water that dried "on its own" has already started corrosion.

## The log

Write the inspection result into the beacon log (see [Beacon Log Template](beacon-log-template)): date, findings, actions. Next season's inspection then has a baseline.
