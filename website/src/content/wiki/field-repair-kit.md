# Field Repair Kit

The beacon's field failures are few and fixable: a snapped antenna, a dead cell, a broken wire. The repair kit is the pouch that fixes all three, sized for a pack.

## The kit

| Item | Fixes |
| --- | --- |
| Spare SMA whip antenna | The most common field failure (see [Antenna Mounting](antenna-mounting)) |
| Spare 18650 cell | Battery failure (see [Battery Field Swap](battery-field-swap)) |
| 30 cm of spare wire | Broken wires (see [Wire Gauge Guide](wire-gauge-guide)) |
| Small soldering iron + solder | Any of the above |
| Heat shrink + tape | Insulation and strain relief |
| SMA wrench / small pliers | Connector work |
| Spare SMA connector | The antenna-to-case joint |

## The 20-minute rule

Every fix in the kit is a 20-minute job at most, done with gloves in a shelter. If a repair needs more than the kit, the plan was wrong, not the repair.

## The skills

The kit is useless without the practice: [Soldering Basics](soldering-basics) for the iron, [Battery Field Swap](battery-field-swap) for the cell, [Homebrew Antenna](homebrew-antenna) for the antenna. Rehearse each repair once at home, and the field version takes minutes.

## The weight

The whole kit weighs under 300 g: the price of converting a beacon failure from "mission over" to "ten-minute stop". For a group, one kit per pair of people is the right density.
