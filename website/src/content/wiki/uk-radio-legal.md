# UK Radio Legal

The UK left the EU but kept the CEPT radio framework, which Ofcom implements. The beacon's 433 MHz ISM operation is license-free within the standard limits.

## ISM operation

The 433.050 - 434.790 MHz band is available for license-free short-range devices under the harmonized SRD rules retained from CEPT. The general-use ceiling is 10 mW ERP. CE/UKCA-marked modules satisfy the equipment side.

## UK specifics

- The UK Framework Agreement retains the ECC decisions, so the beacon's European configuration applies unchanged.
- PMR446 remains license-free at 500 mW ERP with integrated antennas only.
- SEARCH mode (receive-only) requires no license.

## Power, duty cycle and emergency

The same triangle as the rest of CEPT: routine beaconing at or below the ERP ceiling, duty-cycle limits respected, higher settings reserved for genuine emergencies, and the distress exception applying in real emergencies. See [Duty Cycle Rule](duty-cycle-rule) and [Emergency Use Legal](emergency-use-legal).

## Amateur alternative

UK amateur licensing (M6/M0 and higher callsigns) opens the 70 cm band with full privileges and mandatory identification (see [Callsign Identification](callsign-identification)).
