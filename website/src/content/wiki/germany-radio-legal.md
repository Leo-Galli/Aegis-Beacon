# Germany Radio Legal

Germany regulates radio through BNetzA, and like its neighbors it applies the CEPT harmonized framework. The beacon's 433 MHz ISM operation is license-free within the standard limits.

## ISM operation

The 433.050 - 434.790 MHz band is open for license-free short-range devices under the harmonized SRD rules (BNetzA implements the CEPT/ECC decisions directly). The general-use ceiling is 10 mW ERP; CE-marked modules satisfy the equipment requirements.

## German specifics

- The framework is the same one used by IT/CH/FR/AT: a beacon configured for one of them behaves identically in Germany. The [Frequency Compatibility](frequency-compatibility) tables apply unchanged.
- PMR446 operation is license-free at 500 mW ERP with integrated antennas only.
- Receiving (SEARCH mode) requires no license anywhere.

## Power and duty cycle

Routine beaconing at or below the ERP ceiling, duty-cycle limits respected, higher settings reserved for genuine emergencies. The firmware's defaults and the [Regulatory Compliance](regulatory-compliance) page are written to this standard.

## The emergency exception

The distress exception applies under German law as across CEPT: in a genuine emergency, the normal limits do not prevent you from summoning help. EMERGENCY mode exists for this case; see [Emergency Use Legal](emergency-use-legal).

## Amateur alternative

German amateur licensing (DL/DO callsigns) opens the 70 cm band with full privileges and mandatory identification (see [Callsign Identification](callsign-identification)).
