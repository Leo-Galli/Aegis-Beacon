# Field Checklist

The bench checklist proves the beacon works; the field checklist proves it works HERE. This page is the field sequence.

## The deploy

| Step | Check |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Payload current: identity, coordinates (see [Config Payload Format](config-payload-format)) |
| 2 | Channel scanned and chosen (see [Scanning the Band](scanning-frequency-table)) |
| 3 | Beacon deployed: antenna up, clear of metal and snow (see [Field Deployment](field-deployment)) |
| 4 | Confirmation: walk 100 m, one burst decoded (see [Signal Strength Mapping](signal-strength-mapping)) |

## The mission checks

| Interval | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Every hour | Battery reading trend (see [Battery Monitor Details](battery-monitor-details)) |
| Every waypoint | The group check-in (see [Group Communication Plan](group-communication-plan)) |
| After any fall or soak | Seals, antenna, one test burst (see [Cleaning and Inspection](cleaning-and-inspection)) |
| At temperature change | The cold battery rules (see [Cold Weather Batteries](cold-weather-batteries)) |

## The emergency trigger

If the beacon must switch to EMERGENCY mode: confirm identity, confirm coordinates, confirm GPS or enter manual (see [Emergency Response Minute by Minute](emergency-response-minute-by-minute)).

## The end

| Step | Check |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Beacon off or back to routine mode |
| 2 | Log the session (see [Beacon Log Template](beacon-log-template)) |
| 3 | Charge for the next trip (see [Battery Storage](battery-storage)) |
