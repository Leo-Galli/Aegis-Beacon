# Night Operations

Night removes the visual toolbox: mirrors, ground signals, and aircraft spotting are gone. The radio becomes the primary signal, and the beacon's design suddenly makes full sense.

## What changes at night

| Tool | Day | Night |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beacon | One signal among many | The primary signal |
| Mirror | 15 km | Useless |
| Ground signals | km (visual) | Useless |
| Whistle | Local | Local, but carries differently in cold air |
| Fire | Visible | More visible |

## The night radio picture

Radio propagation changes modestly at night (see [Tropospheric Ducting](tropospheric-ducting) for the atmospheric side), but the practical difference is quieter bands: fewer consumer devices, so the beacon's channel is cleaner. The [Scanning the Band](scanning-frequency-table) sweep often finds clearer channels at night.

## The audio cues

At night, listening replaces looking:

- The burst rhythm: regular timing means a beacon, not interference (see [Firmware TX Scheduler](firmware-tx-scheduler)).
- The tone's loudness guides the last meters (see [Homing Technique](homing-technique)).
- The [Whistle Signals](whistle-signals) reply becomes the confirmation that a human is near.

## The battery note

Night missions run longer and colder. The [Winter Pack](winter-pack) rules apply double: spare cells against the body, conservative power settings, and the [Battery Field Swap](battery-field-swap) skill rehearsed.
