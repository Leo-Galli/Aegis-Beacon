# Morse Audio Cheat Sheet

The beacon's transmissions are tones, and the receiving skill is audio. This page is the sound of the beacon, described so you recognize it.

## The sound

The beacon keys a 700 Hz tone (see [Audio Tone Details](audio-tone-details)):

- A dot: a short beep
- A dash: a beep three times longer
- The gaps: one unit between elements, three between characters, seven between words

## The timing at the beacon's speeds

| Speed | Unit length | SOS duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 WPM | 100 ms | ~2.4 s |
| 20 WPM | 60 ms | ~1.4 s |
| 30 WPM | 40 ms | ~1.0 s |

## The recognition shortcuts

| Pattern | Sound like | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `... --- ...` | dit-dit-dit DAH-DAH-DAH dit-dit-dit | SOS |
| `-.. .` | DAH-dit-dit DIT | DE ("from") |
| A long string of digits | Regular dit-dah groups | Coordinates |

## The listening practice

Tap along with the website [Demo](/demo) until the rhythm is physical (see [Morse Keying Practice](morse-keying-practice)). The goal: SOS is recognized instantly, without counting.
