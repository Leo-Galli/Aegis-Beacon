# Case Cutouts Guide

Cutting the enclosure well takes one careful afternoon. Measure twice, cut once, and deburr everything.

## The five openings

| Opening | Size | Position |
|---------|------|----------|
| OLED window | Matches the display active area | Top face, centered |
| 4 button holes | 6-7 mm each (for 6x6 mm switches) | Below the display, evenly spaced |
| SMA hole | 6.35 mm (1/4") or the SMA flange | Side or top, away from the display |
| 3.5 mm jack hole | 6 mm | One side |
| USB access (optional) | Slot for the USB-C plug | Bottom or side |

## OLED window

- The 2.42" SSD1309 has a glass active area of about 55x27 mm. Cut the window slightly larger (58x30 mm) so the glass does not touch the plastic.
- Cut from the inside where possible, or use a template on the outside.
- Use a hobby knife for plastic (score and snap) or a rotary tool with a cutoff wheel. Sand the edges square.

## Buttons

- 6x6 mm switches need 6.5-7 mm holes; a 7 mm drill bit is perfect.
- Space them at least 10 mm apart so fingers can hit them without pressing two at once.
- The switch caps should poke 1-2 mm above the case surface.

## SMA

- The E22's SMA connector is a panel-mount or a through-hole socket. If it is on the module, the case needs a hole aligned with it and the module must be positioned so the connector reaches the hole.
- Alternatively, use an SMA pigtail: mount the jack in the case, run a short cable to the module.

## The golden rules

- **Deburr every hole**; plastic shavings inside a case find their way into the buttons.
- **No sharp edges** where wires run; add a rubber grommet for the SMA and jack if they pass through metal.
- **Test-fit before final assembly**: close the case, check the buttons travel, the display centers, and the antenna threads fully.

## Waterproofing

Any cut hole is a water path. For IP-rated builds, cover the buttons with a membrane and seal the SMA with a rubber washer - see the waterproofing page.
