# Acknowledgements

No project is an island. The beacon stands on the shoulders of libraries, datasheets, communities and testers, and this page names them.

## The software stack

| Contribution | From |
| --- | --- |
| The SX1262 driver | The RadioLib project and its maintainers |
| The ESP32 Arduino core | The Espressif team and contributors |
| The SSD1306 display driver | The Adafruit and community libraries |
| The build toolchain | PlatformIO and the Arduino ecosystem |

## The radio heritage

The SX1262 comes from Semtech, whose datasheets and app notes (see [SX1262 Datasheet Notes](sx1262-datasheet-notes)) define the radio's behavior. The Morse standard the beacon transmits is over a century old and belongs to no one: it is the most battle-tested signaling protocol in existence.

## The communities

- The amateur radio community, whose operating traditions and regulatory framework shape the beacon's legal design
- The mountain rescue community, whose field practice this wiki documents
- The open source hardware community, for the culture of sharing build knowledge

## The testers

The people who took prototypes into the field, broke them, and reported what broke: their findings are embedded in the troubleshooting pages, the firmware fixes, and the roadmap.
