# Pack Check Routine

Five minutes before leaving the house beats an hour of troubleshooting in the field. This page is the routine, in order, timed.

## The five-minute routine

| Minute | Check |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Battery voltage (see [Battery Monitor Details](battery-monitor-details)) |
| 2 | Payload review: callsign, message, coordinates current |
| 3 | One test transmission decoded by the second beacon or SDR |
| 4 | Case seals, antenna mount, buttons |
| 5 | Spare cell, whistle, mirror, log sheet (see [Pre Trip Checklist](pre-trip-checklist)) |

## The payload check matters most

A beacon transmitting last year's coordinates is worse than silence: it sends the team to the wrong place. Verify the payload shows the CURRENT mission's identity and, if GPS is off, the CURRENT manual coordinates. The [Config Payload Format](config-payload-format) page explains what to look for.

## The transmission check

Fire one burst and decode it. This single test catches: dead battery, dead radio, wrong channel, wrong payload, broken antenna. It takes 30 seconds and it is the highest-value check in the routine.

## The seasonal reset

After any long storage, the routine becomes a session (see [Beacon Care Seasonal](beacon-care-seasonal)): charge cycle, full bench test, seal inspection. A beacon that lived in a drawer for six months is not the beacon you tested in June.
