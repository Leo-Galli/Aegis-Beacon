BEACON SETTINGS
IDENTITY (NAME IN MORSE)
When enabled, the beacon transmits:
SOS DE [FIRSTNAME] [LASTNAME]
Only capital letters A–Z are transmitted.
Keep names short to minimise TX time.
GPS (NEO-6M MODULE)
NO FIX
GPS: RX=GPIO34 TX=GPIO12 9600 baud
Format: SOS PSN N4553 E01230
N4553 = 45°53' N — concise for Morse.
Stale fix (post deep-sleep) still transmitted.
BUTTON CONTROLS (v5.3)
SW_SEL — GPIO32
Short press: toggle VOL / WPM adjust target.
Long 3s: enter Config (WiFi AP mode).
OLED overlay shows active target.
SW_UP — GPIO35
Increment: Volume +10 or WPM +1.
Auto-repeat after 500ms hold.
Updates live, no reboot needed.
SW_DN — GPIO34
Decrement: Volume -10 or WPM -1.
Auto-repeat after 500ms hold.
Vol range: 20-255. WPM range: 5-40.
